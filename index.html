SMC — Terms & Conditions

1. Zero Fake Tasks: Creating false destinations, dummy round trips, or inflating distance (km) is strictly prohibited and results in immediate ID suspension.

2. Genuine GPS & Timing: Using fake GPS/mock-location apps or marking a task complete without reaching the destination is logged as fraud.

3. Honest Attendance: Runners must only turn ON DUTY when physically ready to work. Proxy attendance for another employee is forbidden.

4. Bank Document Security: Cheques, KYC files, and branch pouches must be handled safely and handed only to authorized branch staff.

5. BM / DBM Audit Authority: Branch Manager and Deputy Branch Manager hold final authority to verify, approve, reject, or escalate any task or leave request.
